# Spatial Yellow-TLS analysis contract

- Independent unit: patient.
- Expected scope: 10 patients, 12 sections, and 23,882 QC-retained spots.
- Primary quantities: within-section Spearman association of complete
  Yellow-module activity with TLS10, followed by patient-level aggregation.
- Primary uncertainty: patient-level model-based interval and exact sign-flip
  test; spots and sections are not promoted to independent patients.
- Composition sensitivity: patient-level partial association using the
  registered RCTD-derived covariates.
- Post-result sensitivity: Yellow-minus-CD79A versus TLS9 is labelled as such
  and is not treated as prespecified confirmation.
- Claim ceiling: expression-based spatial context only; not sparse4 spatial
  validation, histological TLS confirmation, or causal evidence.
